# Walkthrough script: under 5 minutes, camera on

Record with Loom (camera bubble on) at full-screen browser width. Open these tabs before you start:

1. https://ashboi-cyber.github.io/fathom-rebuild/meetings/q4-planning/
2. https://ashboi-cyber.github.io/fathom-rebuild/
3. https://ashboi-cyber.github.io/fathom-rebuild/live/
4. A private/incognito window, kept empty, for opening a shared clip.

Speak naturally. The lines below are a guide, not a script to read word for word.

---

### 0:00–0:30 · The call I made

> "First I used the real Fathom on a Google Meet call. Two things stood out: it heard '8x' as 'ATX' and put that into an action item, and searching for a word I'd said, 'GitHub', found nothing.
>
> This is Sounding, my rebuild of Fathom. The brief said the case that matters is an eight-person call that runs an hour, so that's where I spent the time. I faked the recording bot, which the brief allows, and I'll show exactly where. Everything on top of the recording is real and working."

**On screen:** tab 1, the Q4 Planning meeting.

### 0:30–1:30 · The hour-long, 8-person call

> "This is a 62-minute planning call with eight people. In Fathom you get a video and a long transcript. Here, under the player, the timeline is coloured by who's speaking, and the meeting is split into chapters, so I can jump straight to 'The live ETA collision', where it got heated."

- Hover over the timeline to show the tooltip, then click **The live ETA collision** in the Chapters list.
- Press play (space bar). Point at the lit-up tile and the captions.

> "Playback is simulated from the transcript timings. That's the stubbed capture layer. The transcript follows along."

- Switch to **Speakers** under the player.

> "This is the view built for an eight-person call: one lane per person, showing exactly when they spoke. Kenji spoke 6% of the time, but he raised the biggest risk. I click his name and the transcript shows only what he said."

- Click **Kenji Watanabe**.

### 1:30–2:15 · Notes and action items

- Open **Summary**, then switch the template to **Decision log**.

> "The summary starts with a short version, then the detail. The template picker marks the best fit for each kind of meeting. This one lists every decision, who made it and why, and every point links to the second it was said."

- Click a timestamp, then open **Action items** and pick one person from the dropdown.

> "Action items have owners and due dates, and each links to the moment it was agreed."

### 2:15–3:00 · Clip and share with someone who wasn't on the call

- Open **Transcript**, select a sentence with the mouse, and click **Clip this**.
- Open **Clips**, click **Share**, then **Copy link**.
- Paste the link into the incognito window.

> "Here's what someone who wasn't on the call sees. There's no login. It's just that moment, with the rest of the meeting shaded out, plus the transcript and notes."

### 3:00–3:40 · Search across every meeting

- Tab 2 (home). Press **Ctrl K** and type **live ETA**.

> "Search covers every word anyone said in every meeting, plus the notes. The live-ETA promise comes up in nine of the ten calls, and I can narrow it to what Tom said."

- Click **Tom**, open a result, and show it landing on the exact line.

### 3:40–4:25 · Highlighting mid-call

- Tab 3 (/live). Click **Fast-forward the demo**, press **H** once, wait a few seconds, then press **A**.

> "This is a live call, replayed. When something matters I press H. The highlight reaches back to when that person started talking, so I don't lose the start of the sentence."

- Click **End call and get notes**.

> "When I hang up, it processes the call and my highlights land here, in that meeting's clips, at the right moments."

### 4:25–4:55 · What I left out, and how I worked

> "I left out CRM sync, teams, billing and transcript editing. They're real features, but none of them makes the hour-long call easier to use. The AI notes were generated ahead of time because this is a public static site with no API key. The repo has the full agent log in `.agent-logs`, committed as I went, and the README explains every decision. Thanks for watching."

---

**Before you hit stop:** check that the video is under 5:00 and your camera was on the whole time.
