# Landing page brief: Sounding

A rewrite of a cinematic SaaS landing prompt (written for "PULSE", an AI churn tool), turned into a brief for Sounding.

## Product
Sounding sits in on your calls, writes down every word with a name on it, and draws the whole conversation as one picture: the sonar print.

## The three "clips", made live instead of filmed
The source prompt asked for three Seedance 2.0 video clips from Higgsfield. At 1080p and 8 seconds each, one clip costs 72 credits, and the account had under 10. So each clip is a real-time scene, which also stays sharp at any screen size and reacts to scrolling.

1. **Hero, "the sounding".** Thousands of particles swirl in a dark sea as a three-armed eddy. As you scroll, they settle into the sonar print of a real seeded call: Q4 planning, 8 people, 66 minutes, 298 turns. Each point lands on the arc of the turn it belongs to, in the order the call happened. When the print is complete, the particles hand over to the crisp drawing. A sodium sweep starts turning and the print beats like a heart, twice and then a rest.
2. **"The signal".** The page pins while the call's transcript glides past at an angle, then straightens. The section has three beats:
   - **Record:** the lines are written in.
   - **Find:** a search types "docks", the stream stops, and one word lights up.
   - **Share:** two lines lift out as a clip card: "They’ll see these 13 seconds, nothing else."
3. **"The calm".** The surface at last: still water, one sounding line with a sodium weight, and rings spreading out. Beneath the water, faintly, is the same print the page opened with.

## Sections
1. **Hero.** "Nothing said gets lost." in massive Big Shoulders. Buttons for "Open the demo" and "Play this call".
2. **Social proof.** Lines actually said in the demo calls, drifting in two rows. Each one opens the recording on that second.
3. **How it works.** Pinned over scene 2, with Record · Find · Share each revealing one line.
4. **Counters.** Real numbers counted from the demo workspace: words written down, action items, hours of talk, turns.
5. **Surfacing.** Light arrives from above and bubbles rise as the page climbs out of the deep.
6. **"This isn’t a screenshot."** The real meeting page, running live inside a browser frame that tilts up into place.
7. **Pricing.** Three tiers (Solo, Crew, Harbor) with the middle one highlighted, glass cards and a monthly/yearly switch. It's labelled as demo pricing.
8. **Questions.** An accordion of plain answers, including an honest one about what the demo simulates.
9. **Final call to action.** "Just talk. We’ll keep it." set over scene 3.

## Design
- **Colour:** the page starts in the app's own sea-ink dark and fades to pale surface light for the body. Sodium yellow (#ebc95c) is the single accent, not violet: it's the brand's buoy light.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders for display, Familjen Grotesk for text.
- **Glass:** reserved for the nav pill and the pricing cards, where there's colour behind for it to catch.
